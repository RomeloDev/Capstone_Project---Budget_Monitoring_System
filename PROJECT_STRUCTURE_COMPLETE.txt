KEY BUDGET MODELS:

Core Budget Hierarchy:
- ApprovedBudget (fiscal year budget)
  - BudgetAllocation (department allocation)
    - DepartmentPRE (receipts & expenditures plan)
      - PRELineItem (individual budget lines, Q1-Q4)
      - PRECategory, PRESubCategory (categorization)
    - PurchaseRequest (procurement requests)
      - PurchaseRequestItem (individual items)
      - PurchaseRequestAllocation (link to PRE)
    - ActivityDesign (activity/event budgets)
      - ActivityDesignAllocation (link to PRE)

Supporting Documents:
- SupportingDocument (for ApprovedBudget)
- DepartmentPRESupportingDocument (for PRE)
- PurchaseRequestSupportingDocument (for PR)
- ActivityDesignSupportingDocument (for AD)
- PRDraft, ADDraft (storage before submission)
- PRDraftSupportingDocument, ADDraftSupportingDocument

Audit & Workflow:
- AuditTrail (comprehensive logging)
- RequestApproval (approval tracking)
- SystemNotification (user notifications)

Realignment:
- Budget_Realignment (traditional realignment)
- PREBudgetRealignment (PRE-based realignment)

BUDGET TRACKING:

BudgetAllocation fields track:
- pre_amount_used: total in approved PREs
- pr_amount_used: total in approved PurchaseRequests
- ad_amount_used: total in approved ActivityDesigns
- remaining_balance: allocated_amount - (pr + ad used)

PRELineItem tracks quarterly consumption:
- q1_amount, q2_amount, q3_amount, q4_amount
- get_quarter_consumed(): Sum of PR + AD allocations
- get_quarter_available(): amount - consumed

DOCUMENT WORKFLOWS:

All documents follow standard progression:
Draft → Pending → Partially Approved → Approved
                ↘ Rejected ↗

Three approval levels:
1. End user submits document
2. Approving Officer reviews and approves/rejects
3. Admin uploads signed copy and finalizes
